Privacy Policy

Meelu Crypto Asset Manager

Last updated: February 2025

1. Introduction

At Crafting Bytes, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Meelu — our Crypto Asset Manager — handles your information. Our core principle is simple: your data stays on your device.

2. Information We Collect

Meelu is designed with privacy as a foundation:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information such as names, email addresses, or contact details.
  • Local Storage Only: All portfolio data, exchange credentials, and settings are stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms.
  • No Tracking or Analytics: We do not use tracking technologies, behavioral analytics, crash reporting services, or advertising identifiers.

3. How We Use Information

Since Meelu does not collect personal data, no information is used for any purpose beyond running the app locally on your device. All data processing — including portfolio calculations, price lookups, and exchange balance fetching — happens on-device or via direct, encrypted API calls you authorize.

4. Data Security

We implement strong security measures to protect your data on-device:

  • Apple Keychain: Exchange API keys and sensitive credentials are stored using Apple Keychain, the highest-security storage available on iOS.
  • Encryption: Sensitive data is encrypted both at rest and in transit.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not store your data on our servers or any third-party cloud. Private keys never leave your device.

5. Third-Party Services

Meelu integrates with cryptocurrency exchange APIs to fetch portfolio data and prices. When using these integrations:

  • We only request the minimum necessary read-only permissions
  • All API communications are encrypted via HTTPS
  • We do not store API keys or credentials on our servers — they remain only on your device
  • Third-party exchanges are subject to their own privacy policies and terms of service

6. Children's Privacy

Meelu is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used Meelu inappropriately, please contact us.

7. Your Rights

Since Meelu stores no data on our servers, you maintain full control over all your data at all times:

  • All data can be deleted by uninstalling the application
  • You can reset app data through the application settings at any time
  • No data is retained by Crafting Bytes after app removal

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed.

9. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Meelu's privacy practices, please contact us:

Email: privacy@craftingbytes.eu

General Contact: contact@craftingbytes.eu

10. Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the European Union and is designed to be compliant with GDPR requirements. Our commitment to privacy extends beyond legal requirements — we aim to offer the best possible protection for our users.
