Privacy Policy

At Crafting Bytes, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our mobile applications.

1. Information We Collect

We are committed to minimizing data collection. Our applications are designed with privacy in mind:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information such as names, email addresses, or contact details.
  • Local Storage Only: All app data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms.
  • No Tracking: We do not use tracking technologies, analytics, or advertising identifiers.

2. How We Use Information

Since we don't collect personal data, we don't use your information for any purpose beyond providing the core functionality of our applications. All data processing happens locally on your device.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Device Security: All data is stored securely on your device using Apple Keychain and other platform-specific security technologies.
  • Encryption: Sensitive data is encrypted both at rest and in transit.
  • No Cloud Storage: We do not store your data on our servers or in the cloud.

4. Third-Party Services

Our applications may integrate with third-party services (such as cryptocurrency exchanges) through their public APIs. When using these integrations:

  • We only request the minimum necessary permissions
  • All API communications are encrypted
  • We do not store API keys or credentials on our servers
  • Third-party services are subject to their own privacy policies

5. Children's Privacy

Our applications are not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, traditional data rights (such as access, correction, or deletion) don't apply. However, you maintain full control over your data:

  • All data can be deleted by uninstalling the application
  • You can reset app data through the application settings
  • No data is retained after app removal

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

Email: privacy@craftingbytes.eu

General Contact: contact@craftingbytes.eu

9. Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the European Union and is compliant with GDPR requirements. Our commitment to privacy extends beyond legal requirements to ensure the best possible protection for our users.
