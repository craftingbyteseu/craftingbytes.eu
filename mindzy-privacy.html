Privacy Policy

Mindzy — Daily Quiz App

Last updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

At Crafting Bytes, we are committed to being transparent about how Mindzy handles your information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Information We Collect

Mindzy collects the following information to provide and improve the app experience:

  • User Identifier: A randomly generated user ID is assigned to your account. This is not linked to your real identity.
  • Username: A display name you choose when setting up your profile.
  • Game Progress & Statistics: Quiz results, scores, streaks, completed quizzes, and other in-game statistics. This data is stored to allow you to restore your game state across devices or after reinstallation.
  • App Usage Statistics: General, anonymized usage data to help us understand how the app is used and improve its features.
  • Crash Reports: Diagnostic information collected when the app crashes, used solely to identify and fix bugs.

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:

  • To save and restore your game progress and statistics across devices
  • To personalize your in-app experience based on your activity
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and technical issues
  • To analyze general usage patterns and improve the app

We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising purposes.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • Apple Keychain: Sensitive information such as authentication credentials is stored using Apple Keychain, the highest-security storage available on iOS.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted via HTTPS.
  • Access Controls: Access to stored data is restricted to authorized systems only.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Deletion: You can delete your account from within the app. Upon deletion, all data associated with your account will be permanently removed from our systems.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Mindzy evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Mindzy's privacy practices, please contact us:

Email: privacy@craftingbytes.eu

General Contact: contact@craftingbytes.eu

8. Jurisdiction

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of the European Union and is designed to comply with GDPR requirements.
