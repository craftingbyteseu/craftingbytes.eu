Data Sources

Where WealthSnap's market data comes from

Last updated: October 2026

1. About This Notice

The balances, holdings and transactions in WealthSnap are the ones you enter. The app adds market data to them: prices, exchange rates, logos and public holiday dates. This page names the sources of that data and gives the attribution each source asks for.

The app requests this data through our own service. For what that service receives, see the WealthSnap Privacy Policy.

2. Public Holidays

  • OpenHolidays API: Contains information from OpenHolidays API, which is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

WealthSnap uses nationwide public and bank holidays only, to count business days.

3. Logos

  • Company and fund logos: Logos provided by Logo.dev.
  • Cryptocurrency logos: Provided by CoinMarketCap.

All logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Their display in WealthSnap identifies an asset and does not imply endorsement.

4. Cryptocurrency Prices

The cryptocurrency list, current prices and price history come from CoinMarketCap.

5. Exchange Rates and Precious Metals

Currency exchange rates and precious-metal reference rates come from Frankfurter, an open-source service that publishes daily reference rates from central banks and other public sources.

6. Stocks and ETFs

Stock and ETF search, quotes and price history come from third-party financial data providers.

7. Accuracy

Market data is provided for information only. It can be delayed, incomplete or incorrect, and it is not financial advice. Check a price with your bank, broker or exchange before you act on it.

8. Contact

If you are a data provider and have a question about how WealthSnap uses or credits your data, please contact us:
